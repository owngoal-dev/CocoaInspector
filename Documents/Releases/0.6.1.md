Inspector 0.6.1 runs the process list from edge to edge, keeps its column headings in sight, and moves its count up to the title.

- The process list drops its inset cards. Each row now runs the full width of the screen, so the columns get the room the card margins used to take.
- The column headings stay pinned at the top while the list scrolls, so every figure stays under the heading that names it, however far down you are.
- "120 processes · Paused" moved from above the columns to under the title in the navigation bar, and the list starts one line higher. On iOS 26 it is the bar's own subtitle; on earlier systems a two-line title looks and reads the same.
- At the accessibility text sizes, where rows stack their figures and have no columns, the heading row is hidden.
